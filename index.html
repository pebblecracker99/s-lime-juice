What's S-Lime Juice?

It's really simple. It's a combination of a little bit of salt, shredded cucumber, and lime juice.

Is it good for you?

Well there's 4 ingredients. 1: Water... (water is pretty good for you)

2. Salt

Salt is yummy and if you sweat a lot doing intense stuff, like working out, or partying, then you get a little weird without enough.

3. Lime

Salt and water alone is gross but when you add lime, the salt balances the bitterness and it becomes veeery interesting.

4. Cucumber

I like it when my food/drink has fun texture and the cucumber is there to do that. Also cucumber and salt is yummy too. There's just a lot of fun combinations in s-lime juice.
