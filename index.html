What S-Lime Juice is

A salt-cucumber limeade designed for heat, hydration, and refreshment. Built for events, festivals, and hot summer days where people want something simple, fresh, and functional.

Why it actually works.

Four ingredients. Ingredient 1: Water... :p

2. Salt

When you sweat in the heat, you lose sodium alongside water. Plain water can't replace it — it actually dilutes what's left. The salt in S-Lime restores that balance so your cells absorb and hold fluids properly.

3. Lime

Lime brings natural acidity, depth, and a dose of Vitamin C. It rounds out the salt and keeps the drink complex and satisfying, which is why no sweetener is needed. It also supports digestion and helps the body absorb iron from food.

4. Cucumber

Cucumber adds a refreshing, low-calorie, water-rich base that makes the drink feel more hydrating and less sugary. The pulp gives a light texture similar to aloe vera drinks.
