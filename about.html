S-Lime Juice Makers

S-Lime Juice started with me and 2 friends I made as a student in Sweden.

We all come from different corners of the world: Me originally Tajik, raised in the Czech Republic; Annaii originally Chilean, raised in Sweden; and Hillary originally Filipino, raised in Belgium.

Coming from households with strong flavours, we realized that there's a real gap in Europe. Europeans like salt — it's in like all their food — so why not add it in drinks and make things a little more interesting?

In Tajikistan Doogh was my favourite.

In Chile they have the Pisco Sour as a national drink

In the Philippines they have Calamansi juice.

We wanted to try making our own and S-Lime Juice came to be.

After piloting our first samples at a start-up bootcamp, we launched at Umeå University’s annual job fair (Uniaden), which was attended by ~4,000 people.

The S-Lime Juice pilot was a hit — 75% of all people who tried it loved it.

After encouragement from friends, family, and S-Lime Juice lovers, we decided to revive the project in Prague.

Where it all comes together

Three friends. Three backgrounds. Three core ingredients. Salt, lime, and cucumber <3 coming together to create something refreshing, simple, and different.
